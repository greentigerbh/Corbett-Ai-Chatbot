Corbett Park Directory

Explore Safari Zones, Wildlife Habits, Timings & Practical Tips

info Quick Facts

  • Established 1936 (As Hailey National Park)
  • Total Area 1,288.31 sq km
  • Altitude Range 360 m to 1,040 m
  • Best Time to Visit November 15 to May 15
  • Key Rivers Ramganga, Kosi, Sonanadi

campaign Wildlife Guidelines

  • ⚠️ Advance Booking: Core zone booking opens 45 days in advance. Carry original matching physical IDs.
  • 🚫 Zero Littering: Plastic is strictly banned inside the reserve. Fines are levied.
  • 🦺 Safety first: Never step out of the safari vehicle under any circumstances except at designated canteen areas.
  • 🤫 Keep Silent: Heavy noises, music, and shouting scare animals. Enjoy nature's silence.

explore Safari Zones In-Depth

Dhikala Zone

Dhikala Gate (Dhangarhi Gate)
MOST FAMOUS & PRESTIGIOUS

The heart of the reserve, Dhikala offers wide open grasslands (chaurs) and incredible riverine views of the Ramganga river. It is the only zone offering mandatory forest lodge stays inside the core jungle. High probability of Tiger, Elephant, and Crocodile sightings.

Open Period 15 Nov - 15 June
Safari Style Canter & Lodge Resident Jeep
Tiger Sightings ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Excellent)
Specialty Grasslands & River Basin

Bijrani Zone

Aamdanda Gate
DAY SAFARI FAVOURITE

Known for its diverse foliage, dry rocky stream beds, and dense sal forest cover. Historically a shooting block, Bijrani is incredibly popular for day jeep safaris because of its high tiger sighting density and close proximity to Ramnagar city.

Open Period 15 Oct - 30 June
Safari Style Morning/Evening Jeep
Tiger Sightings ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very High)
Specialty Mixed Forest & Dry Beds

Jhirna & Dhela Zones

Jhirna & Dhela Gates
OPEN YEAR ROUND 🌿

These two adjacent zones offer fantastic birding, wild elephant sightings, and wild boars. They remain open during the monsoon season when other core zones are closed, making them extremely popular for rainy season travelers.

Open Period All 12 Months
Safari Style Morning/Evening Jeep
Wildlife Sighting ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Good Bear/Elephant)
Specialty Monsoon Access & Grasslands

pets Wildlife Directory

🐯
Royal Bengal Tiger

Corbett holds the highest density of wild tigers in the world. Famous hotspots: Dhikala, Bijrani, Garjiya.

🐘
Asian Elephant

Massive wild herds roam the meadows and river basin. Best seen in Dhikala chaur and Ramganga reservoir.

🐆
Indian Leopard

Extremely stealthy, leopards inhabit the hilly terrains and rocky areas. Best seen in Durga Devi and Sitabani.

🦅
Kumaon Birdlife

Over 600 species of migratory and local birds like Great Hornbills, Crested Serpent Eagles, Fish Eagles.
